## 1. The brief

Its "Who pays, and for what" line: *"Committed local hikers who go out most weekends and plan around conditions pay $6/month, monthly only, no annual plan yet, for the Thursday-night conditions email."* Nine months of history behind it: 240 paying subscribers, $1,440 MRR, 39 consecutive weekly issues. The plan's Offer sentence: *"Committed local hikers in one metro pay $6/month for TrailNotes, a Thursday-night email that reports, trail by trail, whether each trail is clear, muddy, snowed in, washed out or closed this week..."*

Intake wanted five answers and the dump held none of them, so each went under Open unknowns instead of being answered: (1) which metro and state; (2) how "about $340/month, all-in" was arrived at; (3) hours and money beyond the 9-11 logged; (4) which contributors would solo-produce an issue, and on what terms; (5) what covers liability and sales tax. On the fifth: *"No jurisdiction is named, so any statement about local law here is an unverified local-law assumption and routes to the researcher once the state is known."* Three more were noted and not asked, including the basis for expecting 40 annual conversions.
