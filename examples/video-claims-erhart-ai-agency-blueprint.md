Source: "AI Agency Blueprint: How I'd Go From $0 to $10K/Month in 90 Days", Adam Erhart, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XGWSm03C6RQ, 13:12 (length per vidIQ, a vendor figure), transcript via pasted transcript from a licensed service (vidIQ), caption type unknown, no timestamps.

The Position column gives the step the presenter names aloud, or an approximate place, plus the quoted words. Numbers are recorded as the transcript renders them; the transcript may mishear figures.

Tag rule applied: a figure is a vendor claim when its source sells the thing the figure promotes and the video sells it. This video and its description sell a trial link, a snapshot, a roadmap and a community.

## Brief (step 2)

The video pitches a one-person AI agency: sell an AI receptionist to local businesses in one niche, run on HighLevel software (step 1, step 3). Stated math: "25 clients, $400 a month each" makes $10,000 a month (open). The price appears as "$300 to $500" (step 1), "297" (step 3), and "297 or 3.97, sometimes even 4.97" (step 3). Clients pay the agency; the video gives no software cost. Setup takes "5 to 10 minutes" per client and about "15 minutes a month" to maintain (open, step 3). Market figures: "over 36.2 million small businesses in the US" (open); "62% of small businesses miss incoming phone calls"; the first to respond "wins 78% of the time" (step 1). Pace: "two clients a week" reaches 25 "in about 3 months" (open), yet the first client takes "2 to 4 weeks" (close). Warm outreach: "about one in five says yes" (step 4). The operator needs no code, website or logo (steps 3, 4). The presenter claims "three sevenfigure agencies, over 1500 small businesses served, zero employees" (open). A second service, review requests, brings "new Google reviews inside the first two weeks" (step 5). The presenter sells a HighLevel trial through an affiliate link, an "agency OS" snapshot, a 90-day roadmap and a private community (close).

## The three tells

- Does the video sell something: yes. A HighLevel trial through an affiliate link, an "agency OS" snapshot, a 90-day roadmap and a private community (close); the description adds a free course and discloses an affiliate commission.
- Does it show costs beside results: no. The only price stated is what the client pays. No software, usage or ad cost appears anywhere in the video (row 10 is the outside check).
- Does it state a time frame: yes for the projection (two clients a week, 25 in about 3 months, open; first client in 2 to 4 weeks, close). None is attached to the presenter's own agencies (row 1).

## Checked and not checked

Checked this run: the market size (row 3), the software price (row 10), the origins of the two phone-call figures (rows 4, 5). Not checked, left unverified: every result, cost-of-time and operator claim, and the compliance claim in row 14.

## Not stated in the video

The software price, the per-minute usage charge for voice calls, a churn rate, how many of the 36.2 million businesses would buy, and what rules apply to recording or automated voice calls. These are gaps, not claims.
