# Experiment Brief Template

Copy this structure for every experiment. Leave no field blank; write "zero" or "none" rather than omitting a field. Delete this instruction line in the filled copy.

---

**Experiment name:** [short, specific, names the belief being tested, not the initiative]

**Date drafted:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Plan unit or dimension this supports:** [the specific plan row or grading dimension that prompted this test]

**Gate status:** [Cleared to run: business has a recorded owner GO. / Planning support only: no GO yet, this brief may not be executed, scheduled, or resourced.]

**Spend gate:** [Zero-spend test, no owner spend approval required before scheduling. / Spend required: $[amount] cap, stops for owner approval before it runs regardless of gate status above.]

## Hypothesis

If we [do X], then [named segment] will [specific, observable behavior], because [reason]. State it so it can fail.

## Smallest decisive test

What happens: [the exact action taken]
To whom: [the specific population, how it was selected]
Over what channel: [where the test runs]
For how long: [start date to end date, or elapsed duration]

## Budget cap

$[amount]. If zero, state "zero, no spend."

## Time cap

Ends on [date] or after [duration], regardless of how it is going.

## Success metric

[One measurable number or rate. Not a feeling.]

## Minimum sample or decision threshold

[The count of responses, visitors, replies, or orders below which the result is inconclusive rather than negative.]
If the time or budget cap is reached before this threshold: [extend once at the same budget / call it inconclusive and stop]

## Stop rule (mandatory)

What result kills this: [the specific negative outcome that ends this line of work]
What ending this means concretely: [do not repeat this exact test, do not revise and retry a specific variable, do not conclude something broader than the test actually covered]

## Next action by outcome

If the hypothesis holds: [who does what, next unit or step]
If the hypothesis fails: [who does what, next unit or step, or explicit stop]

## Notes and assumptions

[Anything assumed best-effort per the constraints rule, with the assumption named plainly. Jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, or licensing claims are never assumed here; they go through research first and get named as unverified until they have.]
