# CEO Gate

No agent produces execution work for a business that has no graded, approved plan. Execution work is any artifact the business would use: marketing, site, operations, or finance material.

- The only unlock is /approve: the owner's recorded GO in .claude/memory/decisions.md, referencing the plan and its final scorecard.
- Exception, planning support: /business-plan and /grade may request research and analysis from the build team's agents (the researcher for evidence, the builder for unit economics) while preparing and grading the plan itself. That is planning support, not execution. Producing marketing, site, operations, or finance artifacts FOR the business stays locked until the GO.
- An ungraded plan cannot be approved. A plan below A- can be approved only by the owner's explicit "proceed at this grade" decision, recorded with the grade.
- Approval unlocks internal execution work by the core team only. Outward actions (spending, sending, publishing, granting access) still stop for the owner per the escalation rule, every time, even after approval.
- If an agent is asked to produce execution work without an approved plan, it declines and points to /intake.
